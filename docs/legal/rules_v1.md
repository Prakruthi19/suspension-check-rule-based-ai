## Quoted authority

**IL-01** (105 ILCS 5/10-22.6(b), [source](https://www.ilga.gov/legislation/ilcs/fulltext.asp?DocName=010500050K10-22.6)): "Any suspension shall be reported immediately to the parents or guardian of a student along with a full statement of the reasons for such suspension and a notice of their right to a review."

**IL-02** (105 ILCS 5/10-22.6(b), [source](https://www.ilga.gov/legislation/ilcs/fulltext.asp?DocName=010500050K10-22.6)): "...along with a full statement of the reasons for such suspension and a notice of their right to a review."

**IL-03** (105 ILCS 5/10-22.6(b), [source](https://www.ilga.gov/legislation/ilcs/fulltext.asp?DocName=010500050K10-22.6)): "The written suspension decision shall detail the specific act of gross disobedience or misconduct resulting in the decision to suspend."

**IL-04** (105 ILCS 5/10-22.6(b), [source](https://www.ilga.gov/legislation/ilcs/fulltext.asp?DocName=010500050K10-22.6)): "The written suspension decision shall also provide a rationale as to the specific duration of the suspension."

**IL-05** (105 ILCS 5/10-22.6(b-20), [source](https://www.ilga.gov/legislation/ilcs/fulltext.asp?DocName=010500050K10-22.6)): "...may be used only if other appropriate and available behavioral and disciplinary interventions have been exhausted..."

**IL-06** (105 ILCS 5/10-22.6(b-20), [source](https://www.ilga.gov/legislation/ilcs/fulltext.asp?DocName=010500050K10-22.6)): "...the student's continuing presence in school would either (i) pose a threat to the safety of other students, staff, or members of the school community or (ii) substantially disrupt, impede, or interfere with the operation of the school."

**IL-07** (105 ILCS 5/10-22.6(b-15), [source](https://www.ilga.gov/legislation/ilcs/fulltext.asp?DocName=010500050K10-22.6)): "Out-of-school suspensions of 3 days or less may be used only if the student's continuing presence in school would pose a threat to school safety or a disruption to other students' learning opportunities."

**IL-08** (105 ILCS 5/10-22.6(b-25), [source](https://www.ilga.gov/legislation/ilcs/fulltext.asp?DocName=010500050K10-22.6)): "Students who are suspended out-of-school for longer than 4 school days shall be provided appropriate and available support services during the period of their suspension."

**IL-09** (105 ILCS 5/10-22.6(b-30), [source](https://www.ilga.gov/legislation/ilcs/fulltext.asp?DocName=010500050K10-22.6)): "...ensure that suspended students are provided the opportunity to make up work for equivalent academic credit."

**IL-10** (105 ILCS 5/10-22.6(b-10), [source](https://www.ilga.gov/legislation/ilcs/fulltext.asp?DocName=010500050K10-22.6)): "...school boards may not institute zero-tolerance policies by which school administrators are required to suspend or expel students for particular behaviors."

**IL-11** (105 ILCS 5/10-22.6(b), [source](https://www.ilga.gov/legislation/ilcs/fulltext.asp?DocName=010500050K10-22.6)): "...suspend or authorize the suspension of pupils ... for a period not to exceed 10 school days."

**IL-12** (105 ILCS 5/10-22.6(a), [source](https://www.ilga.gov/legislation/ilcs/fulltext.asp?DocName=010500050K10-22.6)): "...the parents or guardian of the pupil must be requested to appear at a meeting of the board, or with a hearing officer appointed by it... Such request shall be made by registered or certified mail and shall state the time, place and purpose of the meeting."

**IL-13** (105 ILCS 5/10-22.6(a), [source](https://www.ilga.gov/legislation/ilcs/fulltext.asp?DocName=010500050K10-22.6)): "The written expulsion decision shall detail the specific reasons why removing the pupil from the learning environment is in the best interest of the school... [and] provide a rationale as to the specific duration of the expulsion."

**IL-14** (105 ILCS 5/10-22.6(a), [source](https://www.ilga.gov/legislation/ilcs/fulltext.asp?DocName=010500050K10-22.6)): "Expulsion shall take place only ... for a definite period of time not to exceed 2 calendar years."

**IL-15** (105 ILCS 10/5 (ISSRA), [source](https://www.ilga.gov/legislation/ilcs/fulltext.asp?DocName=010500100K5)): "A parent or any person specifically designated as a representative by a parent shall have the right to inspect and copy all school student permanent and temporary records of that parent's child."

**IL-16** (105 ILCS 5/10-22.6(b-35), [source](https://www.ilga.gov/legislation/ilcs/fulltext.asp?DocName=010500050K10-22.6)): "...school boards shall create a policy to facilitate the re-engagement of students who are suspended out-of-school, expelled, or returning from an alternative school setting."

**IL-17** (105 ILCS 5/10-22.6(d), [source](https://www.ilga.gov/legislation/ilcs/fulltext.asp?DocName=010500050K10-22.6)): "...a student who is determined to have brought ... a firearm to school ... shall be expelled for a period of not less than one year... The expulsion period may be modified by the superintendent... on a case-by-case basis."

**FED-01** (34 CFR 300.530(d), [source](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-300.530)): "A child with a disability who is removed from the child's current placement... must continue to receive educational services... so as to enable the child to continue to participate in the general education curriculum... and to progress toward meeting the goals set out in the child's IEP."

**FED-02** (34 CFR 300.530(e); 300.536, [source](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-300.530)): "...within 10 school days of any decision to change the placement of a child with a disability because of a violation of a code of student conduct, the LEA, the parent, and relevant members of the child's IEP Team... must review all relevant information in the student's file..."

**FED-03** (34 CFR 300.530(h), [source](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-300.530)): "On the date on which the decision is made to make a removal that constitutes a change of placement... the LEA must notify the parents of that decision, and provide the parents the procedural safeguards notice described in § 300.504."

**FED-04** (34 CFR 300.530(f), [source](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-300.530)): "...return the child to the placement from which the child was removed, unless the parent and the LEA agree to a change of placement..."

**FED-05** (34 CFR 300.530(g); 300.531, [source](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-300.530)): "School personnel may remove a student to an interim alternative educational setting for not more than 45 school days..."

**FED-06** (34 CFR 300.532, [source](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-300.532)): "The parent of a child with a disability who disagrees with any decision regarding placement under §§ 300.530 and 300.531, or the manifestation determination under § 300.530(e)... may appeal the decision by requesting a hearing."

**FED-07** (34 CFR 300.534, [source](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-300.534)): "A child who has not been determined to be eligible for special education... may assert any of the protections provided for in this part if the public agency had knowledge... that the child was a child with a disability before the behavior that precipitated the disciplinary action occurred."

**FED-08** (34 CFR 104.35(a), [source](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-104.35)): "A recipient... shall conduct an evaluation... of any person who, because of handicap, needs or is believed to need special education or related services before taking any action with respect to... any subsequent significant change in placement."

**FED-09** (34 CFR 300.530; OSEP discipline Q&A (2022), [source](https://sites.ed.gov/idea/idea-files/qa-addressing-the-needs-of-children-with-disabilities-and-idea-discipline-provisions/)): "...the use of informal removals... may constitute a disciplinary removal... and count toward the 10-day threshold."
