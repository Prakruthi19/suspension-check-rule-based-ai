# Usability test script (Family Check)

For five 30-minute sessions with parents and LSC members from the ChiEAC
network (Week 6). Synthetic facts only: the participant plays a parent using
the fact card below, never their own situation. Record task completion and
time on task, not people: no names, no recordings of faces or voices.

## Setup (facilitator)

- Open the alpha URL on the participant's own phone if they are willing,
  otherwise on a laptop.
- Say: "We are testing the tool, not you. Think out loud. There are no wrong
  answers. You can stop at any time."
- Hand over fact card B.

**Fact card B** (matches `examples/scenario_b_iep_cumulative_twelve.json`).
Your child is in 4th grade and has an IEP. On Monday, September 21, 2026 the
school says your child was in a fight. On Tuesday you got a paper notice: 7
school days out of school, starting Wednesday. The notice gives the reasons
and the act, but does not explain why 7 days, does not say other
interventions were tried, and says nothing about support services or a
meeting about the IEP. You are not sure it mentioned make-up work. Earlier
this year your child was removed for 5 school days in total, some of them
"just go home for the day" calls.

## Tasks

| # | Task (read aloud) | Success means | Time limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Using the card, fill in what happened. | Form submitted with the card's dates and days | 5 min |
| 2 | What is the most urgent date you need to know about? | Names the MDR deadline from the timeline | 2 min |
| 3 | Is there anything the school seems to have missed? | Finds an "Assert" or "Ask" flag and says it in their own words | 3 min |
| 4 | Where does that come from in the law? | Opens a citation link | 1 min |
| 5 | Get a letter asking for the meeting about your child's IEP. | Opens the MDR request tab | 2 min |
| 6 | Save that letter so you can edit it in Word. | DOCX downloaded | 1 min |
| 7 | What do you still need to fill in before sending it? | Points to the `[brackets]` | 1 min |
| 8 | Put the important dates in your phone's calendar. | `.ics` downloaded and opened | 2 min |
| 9 | You found the notice again: it does mention make-up work. Change that answer to "Yes" and check again. | Re-submits; notices the make-up work flag is gone | 3 min |
| 10 | Who could you call for help? | Finds the resource card | 1 min |

## After the tasks (5 minutes)

1. What was confusing?
2. Was there a word you did not understand?
3. Would you send this letter? Why or why not?
4. On a scale of 1 to 5, how sure are you about what to do next?
