Pattern Check ALPHA · SYNTHETIC DATA ONLY

For Local School Councils, parent groups and reporters. Upload a school's discipline log (CSV or Excel) and see who is suspended, how often, and for what, with three questions to bring to the principal.

Nothing you upload is saved. Name columns are dropped and student IDs are scrambled before anything is counted. Any group with fewer than 10 students is never shown, so no child can be identified.
